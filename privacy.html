What information we collect

This website does not currently provide an online appointment form or accept medical records through a website form. If you contact the clinic by phone, email or WhatsApp, the information you choose to share may be used to respond to your enquiry and coordinate your visit. Please avoid sending sensitive medical records through ordinary email or messaging.

How we use it

The clinic may use contact details and the information in your enquiry to respond, coordinate an appointment and support clinic operations. The clinic should only use this information for these purposes and applicable legal or record-keeping requirements. This website does not operate an online booking database.

Who can see it

Information shared by phone, email or WhatsApp may be handled by the clinic team and the relevant communication providers. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices; review their policies before sharing sensitive information.

How long we keep it

The clinic should retain enquiry and appointment information only for as long as needed for communication, care coordination and applicable record-keeping obligations. For questions or to request correction of contact details, contact the clinic directly.

The site also links to external services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps and social networks; their own privacy policies apply when you use them.
