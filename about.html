Our Story

A Chest Specialist For Anantapur Since 2000

S.L.V (Sri Lakshmi Venkateswara) Chest & Allergy Clinic was started in Anantapur in June 2000 by Dr. Sudhindra Rangu.

The Chest and Allergy Department specialises in the diagnosis, treatment and management of respiratory and allergic disorders. We care for asthma, allergic rhinitis, chronic cough, bronchitis, pneumonia, COPD, tuberculosis and other lung-related diseases, as well as food, skin and environmental allergies.

With an experienced specialist, advanced diagnostic facilities and patient-centred care, we focus on accurate evaluation, effective treatment and long-term management to improve respiratory health and overall quality of life.

A clinic with its own laboratory and testing facilities
  • Spirometry
  • Diffusion capacity (DLCO)
  • FeNO
  • Oscillometry
  • Sleep lab
  • Allergy testing
  • Bronchoscopy
  • GeneXpert for TB diagnosis
  • Laboratory
