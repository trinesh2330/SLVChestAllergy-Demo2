About The Clinic

Specialist Care For Lung And Allergy Conditions

The Chest and Allergy Department at S.L.V Chest & Allergy Clinic specialises in the diagnosis, treatment and management of respiratory and allergic disorders.

With an experienced specialist, advanced diagnostic facilities and patient-centred care, we focus on accurate evaluation, effective treatment and long-term management to improve respiratory health and overall quality of life.

  • Experienced chest physician, MD (TB & RD)
  • Advanced lung function and allergy testing
  • Laboratory, X-ray and day-care facilities in the same place
  • Lower-cost laboratory and X-ray services for people in need

Meet The Doctor

Dr. Sudhindra Rangu

MBBS, MD (TB & RD) · Chest Physician & Allergy Specialist

Dr. Sudhindra Rangu has been practising in Anantapur since June 2000. He trained in respiratory intensive care and bronchoscopy at NIMS, Hyderabad, in allergy testing and management at Bhagavan Mahaveer Jain Hospital, Hyderabad, and in the tuberculosis control programme at the National Tuberculosis Institute, Bangalore.

He is a recipient of the “Inspiring Chest Specialists of India” award at the Times Now Doctor’s Day Conclave.
