Lung Function Tests

Spirometry, DLCO, FeNO & Oscillometry

Breathing tests show how well your lungs are working and help the doctor diagnose and monitor conditions such as asthma and COPD. The clinic offers several different tests so the right one can be chosen for you.

  • Spirometry: measures how much air you can breathe out and how fast
  • Diffusion capacity (DLCO): checks how well oxygen passes from the lungs into the blood
  • FeNO: measures nitric oxide in your breath, a marker of airway inflammation
  • Oscillometry: measures airway resistance during normal breathing

Allergy Testing & Management

Find Your Triggers, Control Your Symptoms

Allergies can affect the nose, chest and skin, and can be linked to food or to things in your surroundings. Dr. Sudhindra Rangu trained in allergy testing and management at Bhagavan Mahaveer Jain Hospital, Hyderabad.

Bronchoscopy

Bronchoscopy & Interventions

In bronchoscopy, a thin flexible tube with a camera is passed into the airways so the doctor can look at the lungs directly and, when needed, take samples or carry out an intervention.

Dr. Sudhindra Rangu trained in bronchoscopy and interventions at NIMS, Hyderabad, and the clinic has a video bronchoscopy system.

  • Direct view of the airways
  • Samples for diagnosis when needed
  • Suitable for persistent cough and other unexplained lung problems, as advised by your doctor

Sleep Lab

Sleep Lab & Sleep Studies

Loud snoring, pauses in breathing during sleep, or feeling very tired in the daytime can be signs of a sleep-related breathing problem. The clinic has a sleep lab to assess these.

  • Assessment of snoring and sleep-related breathing problems
  • Equipment for sleep studies
  • Guidance on treatment after the results
